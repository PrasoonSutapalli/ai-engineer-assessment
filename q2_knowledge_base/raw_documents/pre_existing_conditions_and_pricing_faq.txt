FAQ 1: Smoker and Tobacco Surcharge Explanation
Question: Why is the quoted premium higher for tobacco or smoking users?
Answer: Tobacco users (cigarettes, cigars, vapes, or chewing tobacco) are assessed at higher actuarial morbidity risk. A 20% to 35% premium loading is applied based on age and daily consumption. However, if the policyholder completes a certified smoking cessation program and tests negative on cotinine screen during policy renewal after 12 months, the loading can be formally removed.

FAQ 3: Pre-Existing Disease Coverage Nuances
Question: Are treatments for chronic hypertension and diabetes covered from day one?
Answer: Outpatient regular consultations and daily maintenance medications are covered under the optional OPD Care rider from day one. Inpatient hospitalizations directly resulting from pre-existing diabetes or hypertension complications require the standard 24-month continuous waiting period, unless the 1-Year Buyback Rider was opted at policy inception.

UNDERWRITING RULES & ELIGIBILITY:
1. Entry Age Criteria:
- Minimum entry age for primary policyholder is 18 years.
- Maximum entry age for new policy enrollment is 65 years. Lifelong renewability is guaranteed once enrolled.
- Dependent children are covered from age 91 days up to 25 years.

2. Medical Checkup & Pre-Policy Screening:
- For applicants aged up to 55 years with no declared adverse medical history: Tele-underwriting / No mandatory pre-policy medical checkup (PPMC) required.
- For applicants aged 56 years and above, or any applicant declaring chronic conditions (Cardiac history, Stage 2 Hypertension, BMI > 32, or Insulin-dependent Diabetes): A 100% company-sponsored pre-policy medical screening is mandatory.

3. Claims & Non-Network Hospital Cashless Reimbursement:
- Network Hospitals: 100% Cashless hospitalization across 8,500+ empanelled partner hospitals with pre-authorization within 60 minutes.
- Non-Network Hospitals: Cashless Anywhere facility is available if notification is submitted at least 48 hours prior to planned admission, or within 24 hours of emergency admission. For non-network reimbursement claims, standard claim processing turnaround time is 7 working days upon submission of original bills and discharge summary.

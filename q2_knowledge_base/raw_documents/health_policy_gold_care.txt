SECTION 1: PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS - GOLD CARE COMPREHENSIVE
Record ID: UNSTRUCTURED_RAW_01
The Gold Care Comprehensive Plan provides health coverage from $250,000 up to $1,000,000 Sum Insured.

Room Rent Sub-limits:
- For Sum Insured $250,000 to $500,000: Room rent is capped at 1% of Sum Insured per day for standard private rooms, and 2% of Sum Insured per day for ICU charges.
- For Sum Insured $750,000 and above: No room rent capping; single private air-conditioned room or deluxe room is covered at actuals.

Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:
- Standard waiting period for pre-existing conditions (including Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, Thyroid disorders, and Asthma) is exactly 24 months (2 years) of continuous coverage.
- Optional 1-Year PED Buyback Rider is available at a 15% additional premium, reducing the waiting period to 12 months.

Maternity and Newborn Cover:
- Maternity expenses are covered up to $50,000 per delivery after a mandatory 24-month waiting period.
- Newborn baby is automatically covered from Day 1 up to the sum insured until renewal.
